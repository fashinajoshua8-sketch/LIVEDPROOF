LIVED Proof Rules

These rules apply to contributors participating in campaigns on LIVED Proof.

01 · One accountUse your own account. No duplicate or account-farming.
02 · No spamAvoid repetitive, copy-pasted or meaningless posts and replies.
03 · No botsAutomated engagement, scripts and artificial activity are prohibited.
04 · No fake engagementDo not buy or manipulate likes, replies, followers or impressions.
05 · Quality mattersMeaningful engagement, relevance, consistency and useful interaction matter more than volume alone.
06 · Follow campaign rulesEach campaign may have additional requirements that must be followed.
07 · No misleading claimsDo not make false claims about a project, token, product, partnership or returns.
08 · Respect othersNo harassment, threats, impersonation, hate speech or abusive behaviour.
09 · Leaderboard reviewRankings may be reviewed before final rewards are confirmed.
10 · Reward eligibilityOnly contributors who meet the published campaign requirements are eligible.

LIVED Proof — Platform Guide

Master the platform, track your daily rewards, and maximize your campaign earnings with this complete user guide.

🔥 Daily Rewards & Sequential Streaks

Check in every single day to keep your streak alive and claim your +25 XP reward.

  • The horizontal scroll track displays your active check-in journey starting from Day 1.
  • Each day checks off sequentially one-by-one as you claim.
  • Missing a day resets your streak back to the beginning.

👤 Profiles & Handle Isolation

Your progression is entirely personalized and secure:

  • Streaks, XP, and rewards are bound strictly to your unique X handle or connected profile.
  • Data syncs directly and globally via Supabase upserts (`onConflict: 'handle'`), ensuring separate accounts never overwrite each other.

⚡ Campaigns & Independent Contributions

Engage with the ecosystem to earn protocol volume:

  • Browse active campaigns from the main dashboard.
  • Submit your proof links (posts, videos, and engagements) to verify contributions.
  • Track protocol revenue shares (10%) and independent contributor metrics in real-time.
